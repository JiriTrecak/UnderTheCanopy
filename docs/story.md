# The story

Agreed with Jiri, 2026-10-21 (the arc is his; names, the twelve steps and the first three missions are proposals to play and revise). The campaign system is [campaign](campaign.md).

## The forest and the substance

The forest runs on **substance**: a living, liquid light that wells up from the roots, glowing blue to violet. It gives power to everything that touches it, and it has a mind of its own. It is patient.

- **The beetles** never let it into their bodies. They keep it in glass, coil it in machines and live by their engines. They are the forest's engineers, and they think the other races fools.
- **The bees** are a holy order. They take substance only through ritual, which changes their bodies slowly, deliberately, under rule. They are the only ones who understand what it is.
- **The ants** take it raw, and have for generations. Their broods are addicted; their bodies mutate, every generation further: the Bursters, the Honeypots, the Reaver Queens are what substance makes of ants.

## The trilogy

1. **The ants: the fall.** A young queen sees what substance has done to the other broods and sets out to master it, so that no ant has to mutate again. She learns the beetles' glass and the bees' rites, takes what they will not give, and finally walks into **the Wellspring**, the great source beneath the forest, to command it. She believes she has succeeded. The substance has waited for exactly this: it takes her, and through her the whole forest.
2. **The beetles: the ruin.** The forest after the fall: corruption everywhere, the beetles' machines failing or turning, their engineers fighting to survive in a world gone wrong, and asking whether they can keep using substance at all.
3. **The bees: the cure.** The order that understood substance all along: finding how to cleanse it, fighting the corrupted Queen, and ending the corruption.

The ants' art stays bio-magic (living light, blossoms, never corruption) until the fall: the first time the player sees corruption's look is the twist. The Reaver Queen, the ants' mutated ultimate, foreshadows what the Queen becomes.

## Characters

- **Queen Myrrh**, the young queen, the protagonist (hero: `unit.ants.queen`). Myrrh is a resin, like amber: the colony's treasure. Compassionate, fierce, certain. Her goal is good; her pride is her flaw.
- **Vessa**, the Royal Alchemist (hero: `unit.ants.royal-alchemist`). She distils substance in her own body and knows more than she says. Myrrh's guide and enabler.
- **General Varro**, the army-ant marshal (hero: `unit.ants.marshal`). Served Myrrh's mother. Loyal, sceptical, the one who sees what is happening. He survives her, to appear later.
- **Tiv**, a young scout of Myrrh's guard (a Raider): the player's eyes in the first mission.
- **Mother Ashka**, the matron of the Hollow Brood, mutated beyond recognition.
- **Coilkeeper Brannagh**, the beetle savant who keeps the condenser on the Glass Road (hero: `unit.beetles.coilkeeper`).

## Mission 1: The Hollow Brood

Prologue · Mission One. Night falling into dusk-blue; a quiet, beautiful forest that slowly goes wrong.

- **Map**: a journey, Warcraft III's first-mission shape. A root path from the south-west, along a stream, up two ramps through mossy terraces, down into **the Hollow**, a sunken basin in the north-east where the Hollow Brood's nest stands, still lit, wrong. Side clearings with feral camps and sick pools of over-bright substance among withered plants. Playable about 224 × 224.
- **The player**: Queen Myrrh (level 1, cap 3), Tiv, four Raiders, two Needlers. No base, no building.
- **Opening scene**: fade in on Myrrh and her escort already running along the root path. Tiv runs to meet them: the Hollow's watch-lights went dark three nights ago, its scouts never came back. Myrrh: "Then we go and ask them why."
- **Along the way**: feral ants (twisted Raiders, a Burster) as passive camps that wake when approached; a dead sentry, mutated; a sick pool where Myrrh stops ("It shouldn't glow like that"); survivors of the Hollow (an allied player, cowering in a side chamber) who join her when reached (scene, then their units are handed over).
- **The Hollow**: the nest guarded by feral warriors, and **Mother Ashka**, the brood's matron, mutated into a hulking thing (a Reaver Queen). Defeating her wins.
- **Closing scene**: Ashka, lucid for a moment as she dies: "It sang to us… we drank… and it gave and gave." Myrrh kneels: "No ant will drink like this again. I swear it."
- **Objectives**: reach the Hollow; find the survivors; put Mother Ashka to rest. Optional: find Tiv's lost patrol (a cache of items).
- **AI**: feral brood (enemy, Passive camps; Ashka and her guard Base without rebuilding); Hollow survivors (ally, Passive, dormant until found).
- **Tech**: none (no base).

## Mission 2: Founding

Prologue · Mission Two. Morning in a lush glade: flowers, ferns, mushrooms, a clear stream; hope.

- **Map**: a green glade in the middle (the founding site, amber nodes, a substance spring), the feral burrows in the north-east beyond a stream and a ridge, a second, smaller feral den in the north-west. Two approaches to the glade (a ramp from the north-east, a narrow root bridge from the north-west). Playable about 256 × 256.
- **The player**: Myrrh and the Hollow survivors (workers and a few warriors), founding the colony: a Nest from the start, build up.
- **Opening scene**: fade in on the column arriving in the glade; Myrrh plants her standard: "Here. We start again, and we start clean."
- **Middle**: feral waves from the burrows (small, growing); after the first waves, **Vessa** arrives from the west (scene): she has studied substance her whole life, and knows of beetles who hold it in glass without being changed. She joins.
- **Win**: destroy the feral burrows (the waves stop with them). **Lose**: the Nest falls or Myrrh dies with no Royal Chamber to revive her.
- **Objectives**: build the colony (Brood Mound, Soldier Burrow); destroy the feral burrows. Optional: the substance spring (a Substance Siphon on it).
- **AI**: feral brood (enemy, Waves: Raiders and Bursters trained at their burrows, growing, the first after three minutes).
- **Tech**: Nest, Worker, Brood Mound, Soldier Burrow, Raider, Needler, Chitin Works, Mandibles I, Carapace I, Royal Chamber, Substance Siphon.

## Mission 3: The Glass Road

Act I · Mission Three. Clear, cool afternoon; engineered beetle roads, lantern posts, glass tanks glowing violet; the forest bent to order.

- **Map**: the colony's forward camp in a wooded corner (south-west), the **Glass Road** running across the map on a raised, paved causeway, beetle outposts along it, and the **condenser** (a landmark) in a walled beetle station on a plateau in the north-east. A caravan route crosses the map. Playable about 288 × 288.
- **The player**: Myrrh and Vessa, a camp to build up; **General Varro** joins in the opening scene.
- **Opening scene**: Varro waiting at the camp; he served her mother. Myrrh tells him what she means to do. Varro: "Your mother would have called it madness." Myrrh: "My mother is not here."
- **Middle**: Myrrh goes to the station to ask; Coilkeeper Brannagh refuses her from the wall ("Glass is not for those who drink"), and the beetles attack. Beetle waves along the road; caravans to ambush for resources.
- **Win**: break into the station and bring Myrrh to the condenser. **Closing scene**: the condenser in her hands; Vessa: "Now we can learn what it is." Something hums.
- **Objectives**: speak with the beetles; take the condenser. Optional: ambush three caravans.
- **AI**: beetles (enemy, Waves from the War Lodge, growing, Bolters and Shieldbearers, later Breachers; the station Base, rebuilding); caravans (enemy, Passive units following the road).
- **Tech**: adds the Great Nest, Deep Gallery, Fungus Garden, Burster, Major, Honeypot.
