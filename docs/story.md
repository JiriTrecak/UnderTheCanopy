# The story

Agreed with Jiri, 2026-10-21 (the arc is his; the twelve steps and the first three missions are proposals to play and revise). Names must be as memorable as Arthas or Sylvanas: short, singular, their sound fitting the character, and able to survive the fall. The campaign system is [campaign](campaign.md).

## The forest and the substance

The forest runs on **substance**: a living, liquid light that wells up from the roots, glowing blue to violet. It gives power to everything that touches it, and it has a mind of its own. It is patient.

- **The beetles** never let it into their bodies. They keep it in glass, coil it in machines and live by their engines. They are the forest's engineers, and they think the other races fools.
- **The bees** are a holy order. They take substance only through ritual, which changes their bodies slowly, deliberately, under rule. They are the only ones who understand what it is.
- **The ants** take it raw, and have for generations. Their broods are addicted; their bodies mutate, every generation further: the Bursters, the Honeypots, the Reaver Queens are what substance makes of ants.

## The trilogy

1. **The ants: the fall.** A young queen sees what substance has done to the other broods and sets out to master it, so that no ant has to mutate again. She learns the beetles' glass and the bees' rites, takes what they will not give, and finally walks into **the Wellspring**, the great source beneath the forest, to command it. She believes she has succeeded. The substance has waited for exactly this: it takes her, and through her the whole forest.
2. **The beetles: the ruin.** The forest after the fall: corruption everywhere, the beetles' machines failing or turning, their engineers fighting to survive in a world gone wrong, and asking whether they can keep using substance at all.
3. **The bees: the cure.** The order that understood substance all along: finding how to cleanse it, fighting the corrupted Queen, and ending the corruption.

The ants' art stays bio-magic (living light, blossoms, never corruption) until the fall: the first time the player sees corruption's look is the twist. The Reaver Queen, the ants' mutated ultimate, foreshadows what the Queen becomes.

## Characters

- **Queen Aurelith**, the young queen, the protagonist (hero: `unit.ants.queen`). Her name means "golden stone", which is what amber is: the colony's treasure. Compassionate, fierce, certain. Her goal is good; her pride is her flaw. After the fall she is *Aurelith of the Wellspring*.
- **Oleander**, the Royal Alchemist (hero: `unit.ants.royal-alchemist`), named for the flower that is beautiful and poisonous. She distils substance in her own body and knows more than she says: the Queen's guide and enabler.
- **General Korrath**, the army-ant marshal (hero: `unit.ants.marshal`). Served the Queen's mother. Loyal, sceptical, the one who sees what is happening. He survives her, to appear later.
- **Tiv**, a young scout of Aurelith's guard (a Raider): the player's eyes in the first mission.
- **Mother Ashka**, the matron of the Hollow Brood, mutated beyond recognition.
- **Coilkeeper Vosk**, the beetle savant who keeps the condenser on the Glass Road (hero: `unit.beetles.coilkeeper`).

## How the missions are built

Warcraft III's campaign maps are full of small things beside the main road, and these missions follow them: crates at the start with a potion inside; villagers' side quests with an item at the end; creep camps guarding treasure; a single creep hidden in the trees; a gap in a tree line opening onto a secret glade; a silly secret for the curious (Jimmy the Seal); merchants and healing springs. Every map has:

- **a clear main road** the player can always follow, and the objective on it;
- **side places** off the road, each worth the detour: a camp with a drop, a side quest, a merchant, a spring;
- **one or two secrets**, hidden by the shape of the map (a gap in the trees, a ledge, a tunnel behind rubble), with a good reward;
- **one easter egg**;
- **story beats in place**: short letterboxed scenes where the story happens, and lines when the player finds things.

The forest's "corrupted" creatures (the Rotwood Court) stay out of the ant missions: corruption is first seen at the fall.

## Mission 1: The Hollow Brood

Prologue · Mission One. Dusk deepening to blue night; a quiet, beautiful forest that slowly goes wrong. Playable about 288 × 288. The Queen's level is capped at 3.

- **The player**: Queen Aurelith (level 1), Tiv, four Raiders, two Needlers. No base, no building: a hero's journey.
- **The road**: from the south-west, along a stream, up onto a mossy terrace, down its far side, and down into **the Hollow**, a sunken basin in the north-east where the stricken brood's nest still glows.
- **Opening scene**: fade in on Aurelith and her escort already running along the root path. Tiv runs up to meet them: the Hollow's watch-lights went dark three nights ago, and its scouts never came back. Aurelith: "Then we go and ask them why." The camera follows the column as they set off.

Along the way, in order:

1. **The start**: two amber caches by the road (a Resin Salve, a Moon Dew).
2. **A mushroom circle on the road** (Caplings, a Puffcap): the first, easy fight.
3. **The ford**: dew sprites (Puddle Wisps, Dew Sprites) by the water; Moon Dew.
4. **The dead sentry**: a Hollow ant lying by the road, mutated. Aurelith stops: "This was one of Ashka's. Look at its shell." A short scene.
5. **Side quest: the Lost Patrol.** Tiv: his brother's patrol went north-west and never came back. A webbed hollow (Hungry Weblings: Weblings, a Spitter) holds them cocooned. Freeing them: two Raiders join, and the patrol leader gives a Scout's Whistle.
6. **The sick pool**: feral ants (twisted Raiders, a Burster) drinking from a still pool of over-bright substance among withered plants. Aurelith: "It shouldn't glow like that."
7. **The terrace**: thornback foragers (Thorn Slingers) among berry bushes; off the terrace's east edge, a hornets' paper nest (Hornet Raiders: Hornet Guards, Needle Wasps, a Nest Mother): optional and hard at this level; a Thornband.
8. **The survivors**: in a chamber east of the road, behind a collapsed tunnel mouth (rubble to break), survivors of the Hollow: two Raiders and a Needler, who join after a short scene. A mother tells Aurelith what happened: "They drank from the deep pool. Then they stopped sleeping."
9. **Secret: the Moonlit Hollow.** A gap in the tree line north of the terrace, easy to miss, opens onto a tiny moonlit glade with an old shrine and a sleeping Willow Wisp; the shrine holds a **Broodkeeper's Lantern** (an aura that heals allies around the hero).
10. **Easter egg**: a tree frog alone on a lily pad in the stream. Click it ten times: it croaks, hops away, and leaves a Heartseed.
11. **The Hollow**: the way down a single ramp; feral warriors among the pools; the nest, still lit; and **Mother Ashka**, the brood's matron, mutated into a hulking glowing thing (a Reaver Queen). On sight, a scene: Ashka rises from the pool and turns to them, and does not know them.
12. **Closing scene**: Ashka, lucid for a moment as she dies: "It sang to us… we drank… and it gave and gave." Aurelith kneels beside her: "No ant will drink like this again. I swear it." Fade out.

- **Objectives**: reach the Hollow; find the survivors; put Mother Ashka to rest. Optional: find Tiv's lost patrol.
- **Lose**: Aurelith dies.
- **AI**: the feral brood (enemy, Passive: camps that wake when approached; Ashka and her guard in the nest); the survivors (ally, dormant until found, then handed to the player); the forest's creeps (neutral camps).
- **Tech**: none (no base).

**Built** (2026-10-22): `base/campaigns/vanguard.utcc/missions/hollow-brood.utcm`, authored through the editor's MCP by `tools/maps/hollow_brood.py` (blocks, dressing in `hollow_brood_dress.py`, units, camps and the mission section in `hollow_brood_units.py`); the script is its `mission.lua`. Playable 320 × 320 (map 384), Underleaf, from dusk into night: it starts at 18:30 and its 240-minute day (`dayMinutes`, D83) brings deep blue night at about 21:00 after 25 minutes; the Hollow's frame is withered by the map's own dressing (`landscape.scatter`, D80). As built, beside the plan above: the dead sentry staggers out of the ferns and falls in its scene (bodies sink within two seconds, so the Hollow's dead by the road, on the rim and in the Hollow are props: `scenery.hollow-ant-corpse` and `scenery.hollow-ant-husk`); old webs (`scenery.hollow-web-*`, `tools/props/build_webs.py`) and the lost patrol's silk cocoons (`scenery.hollow-cocoon-*`) fill the webbed hollow, and the broodkeepers' shrine, its lantern burning, closes the Moonlit Hollow (`tools/props/hollow_tripo.json`); the feral brood is player 8, white, bled pale; the survivors' chamber holds a sap spring (rest before the Hollow) and a second spring stands by the stream east of the ford; an old burrow beyond the webs and a beetle wreck in the south-east are small extra places; the Reaver Queen starves (Ravenous), so the script keeps Ashka fed until she is seen, then she comes at them already 1,000 health down. `cargo run --release -p canopy-sim --example mission_walk` plays a mission headless along a route as a plain player would (attack-moves, the Queen's heal and Cocoon), which is how its balance was checked: won comfortably with every objective and side place at level 3 (ten units left); along the main road alone, without potions, the lantern or pulling the Queen back, it is won about half the time, so the side places are worth taking.

**Map fixes** (2026-10-22, from the mission check and the autoplay plan): the Moonlit Hollow's neck is widened (the mycena that pinched it now stands in the frame), so the Queen gets past her own escort to the shrine; the survivors' chamber opens the full width of its rubble (12 cells) so she can step in; the archer survivor starts on open ground in the chamber instead of a wedge behind its spring. The plan sends the Queen up alone for the lantern.

## Mission 2: Founding

Prologue · Mission Two. Morning in a lush glade: flowers, ferns, mushroom rings, a clear stream; hope. Playable about 320 × 320. Cap 4.

- **The player**: Aurelith and the survivors (workers and warriors) found the colony in the glade: a Nest from the start, build up.
- **The feral brood**: its main burrows on a ridge in the north-east beyond a stream, reached up a ramp; a smaller den in the north-west across a ravine, reached over a narrow root bridge. Their waves (Raiders, later Bursters) come from those burrows and stop with them.
- **Opening scene**: fade in on the column arriving in the glade; Aurelith plants her standard on a mossy rise: "Here. We start again, and we start clean."
- **Middle scene**: after the second wave, **Oleander** arrives along the west road with two porters: she has studied substance her whole life, and knows of beetles who keep it in glass without being changed. She joins.

Around the glade:

1. **Amber**: two sites in the glade; a third north-east, guarded by Rootbound Guardians (Rootlings, Old Root); a fourth west by the bog pond, guarded by bog toads (Young Toads, Tree Frogs, a Tadpole Spirit).
2. **The substance spring** at the glade's east edge: a Substance Siphon on it (optional objective). Oleander comments on it when she arrives.
3. **A healing spring** in a fern hollow south of the glade.
4. **The travelling merchant**, a pill-bug trader with a cart at the south-east crossroads, selling potions and a Mossweave Wraps.
5. **A mushroom circle** near the glade: early experience.
6. **The hornets' cliff**: a paper nest on a ledge above the stream (hard), dropping a Warcaller's Standard.
7. **Secret: behind the waterfall.** Where the stream drops into the ravine, a ledge leads behind the falling water to a hidden cache: Quickstep Spurs and a Restoration Draught.
8. **Easter egg**: an Alate asleep in a flower at the glade's edge. Click it and it sneezes pollen over everyone nearby.

- **Objectives**: build the colony (a Brood Mound, a Soldier Burrow); destroy the feral burrows (both). Optional: the substance spring.
- **Lose**: the Nest falls, or Aurelith dies with no Royal Chamber standing to revive her.
- **AI**: the feral brood (enemy, Waves trained at their burrows, the first after three minutes, growing); creeps (neutral camps).
- **Tech**: Nest, Worker, Brood Mound, Soldier Burrow, Raider, Needler, Chitin Works, Mandibles I, Carapace I, Royal Chamber, Substance Siphon.

**As built** (2026-10-22, `base/campaigns/vanguard.utcc/missions/founding.utcm`, authored by `tools/maps/founding.py` through the editor's MCP; the script is `tools/maps/founding.lua`): playable 352 × 352 in the Underleaf biome at 9:00. ## Mission 3: The Glass Road

Act I · Mission Three. A clear, cool afternoon; engineered beetle roads, lantern posts, glass tanks glowing violet; the forest bent to order. Playable about 352 × 352. Cap 5.

- **The player**: Aurelith and Oleander at a forward camp in a wooded south-west corner, with a few workers; **General Korrath** joins in the opening scene.
- **The Glass Road**: a raised, paved causeway running from the west edge to the north-east, with two beetle outposts (Hornwatch towers, Shieldbearers) along it and lantern posts the whole way. At its end, on a high plateau, the walled beetle station with the **condenser**.
- **Opening scene**: Korrath waits at the camp; he served Aurelith's mother. She tells him what she means to do. Korrath: "Your mother would have called it madness." Aurelith: "My mother is not here." He kneels.
- **Middle scene**: when Aurelith first reaches the station's gate, Coilkeeper Vosk refuses her from the wall: "Glass is not for those who drink." The beetles' waves start.

Off the road:

1. **The caravans**: beetle supply carts trundle along the road every few minutes, guarded by a pair of Bolters; ambushing one spills amber crates (optional: ambush three).
2. **The toll house**: an abandoned beetle toll house where a travelling merchant has set up shop (a beetle trader this time).
3. **The bog pond** south of the road: bog toads guarding a Restoration Draught.
4. **The briar thicket** east: the Briar Covenant (Briar Hulks, Bloom Witches, Seedcasters) around an old amber site: a fine expansion once cleared.
5. **The sacred grove**: in the far south-east, the Hollow Stag (level 9) and its Barkguards and Willow Wisps guard a ring of old trees. Far too strong at first, a goal for late in the mission: a **Royal Crest**.
6. **Secret: the old tunnel.** Behind rubble at the camp's north edge, an old ant tunnel runs under the causeway and comes up behind the station's west wall: a way in that the beetles have forgotten. Korrath finds the rubble if Aurelith walks past it: "That's ant work. Old ant work." As built (2026-10-22), its far mound comes up in the field outside the west wall: a quick way between the camp and the station, both ways, not into it. Inside the wall the station's guards broke the mound at once, and armies sent home after the gate fell walked through the station to reach it.
7. **Easter egg**: a beetle engineer asleep under a cart by the toll house, snoring; wake him and he grumbles and gives a Moon Dew.

- **Win**: break into the station and bring Aurelith to the condenser. **Closing scene**: the condenser in her hands, humming; Oleander: "Now we can learn what it is." A violet light rises in the glass. Fade out.
- **Objectives**: speak with the beetles; take the condenser. Optional: ambush three caravans.
- **Lose**: Aurelith dies with no Royal Chamber to revive her, or the camp's Nest falls.
- **AI**: the beetles (enemy, Waves from the War Lodge: Shieldbearers and Bolters, later Breachers; the station Base, rebuilding); caravans (enemy, scripted along the road); creeps (neutral camps).
- **The station gate** (as built): `building.neutral.beetle-gate` (16 × 4, 1,800 health) closes the road where it comes up into the forecourt, between the towers of `scenery.beetle-gate-towers`; it belongs to the road's player 5, which never rebuilds. The caravans turn back in front of it. The lodge's waves would be shut in behind it, so the script holds them until it is broken (Korrath: "Break it, and they come out"); broken, the towers stand as an open gateway.
- **Caravan guards** (as built): when both carts of a caravan are broken, its surviving Bolters fall back up the road to the station's gate, fighting what they meet, and stand guard there.
- **Scene cameras** (as built): every line is framed on its speaker, over the listener's shoulder or as a clean single from the speaker's front. The opening cuts over Aurelith's shoulder onto Korrath and over Korrath's onto her, a two-shot for Oleander, and Korrath's last lines and his kneel in profile with the Queen standing over him (`op-korrath`). At the gate the parley crosses the closed gate: Aurelith from the ramp below it (`gate-queen`), from Vosk's height down past it (`gate-over-vosk`); Vosk from the forecourt below him, from either side (`gate-vosk`, `gate-vosk-close`), and over Aurelith's shoulder up past it (`gate-over-queen`).
- **Pacing** (2026-10-22, `mission_playtest` with the campaign's tech, `--mass 18` and the pushes on the road): won on seeds 1–8 between 25 and 32 minutes with the waves as planned (from the gate's fall, every two minutes, two Shieldbearers and two Bolters, a Breacher more every third wave, the station rebuilding); on seeds 9–16 four wins in 25–36 minutes, one stalemate at the station, and three runs where the skirmish AI never broke the gate or its army stuck in the camp.
- **Map fixes** (2026-10-22): the two lantern pairs that narrowed the road before the station stand at its edges (13 cells wide, from 9); the hidden glade's path and its way in are wide enough for the Queen; the old tunnel's camp mound opens east into its corridor, where the Queen and an army can queue (it faced the chamber wall).
- **Autoplay** (2026-10-22): won on seeds 1–12 in 19–40 minutes. The plan walks up to the gate for the parley, sends the Queen home, and before the Great Bastion attack-moves the army through the inner gate and the yard: ordered straight at the bastion, it jammed in the inner gate under the guards.
- **Tech**: adds the Great Nest, Deep Gallery, Fungus Garden, Burster, Major, Honeypot.
