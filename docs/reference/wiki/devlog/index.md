# Behind the Canopy

Building an RTS, one difficult problem at a time.

These are the stories behind *Under the Canopy*: what we wanted players to feel, what we tried, what went wrong, and what we learned. The [game guide](/guide/getting-started) explains how to play. The devlog explains how the game is taking shape.

## 01 · Teaching an army to listen

*September 10, 2026 · Unit control, movement & combat*

A twelve-ant army takes too long to move across empty ground. A sword lands after its damage. Two lines meet at a doorway and refuse to cooperate. Rebuilding control meant following each of those problems beyond the obvious fix.
