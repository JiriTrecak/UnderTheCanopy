# Bees

::: warning Planned faction
Bees are not yet playable. Flight, canopy hives and a nectar/pollen economy are creative directions, not implemented rules.
:::

The mobile lineage above the forest floor: armored, humanoid insect champions with a distinct winged silhouette. Their elevated view is central to the fantasy of seeing a disaster that ground-bound colonies cannot yet comprehend.

## Design direction

The initial concept emphasizes canopy homes, mobility, harassment and map control. Nectar and pollen were proposed as an economic identity. Their relationship to the current amber/wood economy is unresolved; no costs or units should be inferred from the Ant roster.

## Campaign place

The planned final campaign brings the forest's surviving lineages together against the ancient fungal consciousness. Height and speed let the Bees perceive the scale of the crisis and reach places others cannot.

This is the intended culmination of earlier consequences, rather than a separate adventure that resets the world. [World and campaign concepts](/factions/story).
