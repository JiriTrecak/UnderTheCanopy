# Beetles

::: warning Planned faction
Beetles are part of the game's intended world and visual direction. There is no playable Beetle roster, economy, build tree or balance data in the current declarations.
:::

The intended heavy faction: imposing shells, substantial armor, sturdy fortifications and an oversized brute among more normally sized workers. The goal is a warlike but appealing insect civilization, not an army of unmodified real-world insects.

## Design direction

Beetles replaced Termites in the faction lineup during visual development. Fortress-building is a useful direction for their identity, but the old Termite proposal's fungus agriculture must not be treated as a confirmed Beetle mechanic.

Concept art explored a Beetle fort, colony buildings and a larger brute. Those images communicate silhouette and mood; they do not establish recruitable units, combat statistics or costs.

## Campaign place

The middle campaign is intended to inherit the catastrophe caused by the Ants. How Beetle society resists the spreading fungal consciousness still needs its own detailed design.

For current playable content, see [Ants](/factions/ants). For the broader narrative, see [World and campaigns](/factions/story).
