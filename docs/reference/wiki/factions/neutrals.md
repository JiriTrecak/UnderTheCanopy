# Forest neutrals

Neutral entities belong to no player. They include hostile camp creatures, resource sources and dropped hero items. Neutral ownership by itself does not mean hostility: a mine is a gathering target, while a camp has aggression and leash rules.

## Camp creatures

<WikiCatalog section="units" faction="neutral" />

Camps are authored groups with a home, aggro range, leash and optional reward pool. Their members can mix melee and ranged units. Defeating one defender does not claim the whole camp reward; the camp must be cleared.

See the [map atlas](/maps/) for initial camp compositions and the [loot tables](/guide/loot) for rewards.

## Natural resources

Workers gather from neutral [Amber Mines](/buildings/building-neutral-amber-mine) and [Forest trees](/resources/resource-forest-tree). These use the ordinary selection and targeting interface, with capabilities appropriate to their role.
