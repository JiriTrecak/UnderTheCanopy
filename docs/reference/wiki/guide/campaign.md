# Campaign authoring

No campaign missions are currently shipped. Earlier mission prototypes were removed while the game's art and map-authoring direction was rebuilt. Use **Skirmish** for the shipped Echo Isles and Turtle Rock maps.

The engine retains mission scripting, dialogue, objectives and cinematic camera support. See [mission scripting](/development/mission-scripting) to author a new campaign; the presence of these systems does not imply that old chapters are still available.
