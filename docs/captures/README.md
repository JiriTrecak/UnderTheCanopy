# Review captures

Side-by-side images for Jiri's review (D24): the old game on the left, the new renderer on the right, same map, camera pose and hour. Produced with `just shot` and the old game's reference stage (`docs/renderer.md` §10).

- `m3-terrain/`: renderer core (terrain, scenery, buildings, lighting, shadows). Units, water and effects come in later steps.
- `m4-ui/`: UI toolkit widget gallery in the old game's style (not yet wired into the game).
- `m4-screens/`: the game’s screens (D38). Side by side, old game left (the browser at 1600×900, saved at half size in `old/`) and ours right: `main-menu`, `campaign`, `missions`, `skirmish`, `multiplayer` (the old one lists the deployed server's rooms), `settings`, `loading`, `match` (the old one with its HUD; ours has the play module's HUD still to come), `game-menu`, `confirm-leave`. Ours alone in `new/` (`canopy capture-screens`, 1600×900): `boot`, every settings tab (`settings-keys` shows a refused rebinding), `skirmish-race-select`, `multiplayer-create`, `lobby` (joined to a room on a local copy of the old server, with measured round trips), `match-chat`, `match-desync`, `mission-hud-sample` and `mission-cinematic-sample` (a sample mission state: no mission maps are mirrored), `game-menu-save`, `game-menu-saved`, `game-menu-leave`, `saves`, `saves-main-menu`, `confirm-delete`, `result-defeat`, `result-victory`.
- `m3-water/`: water, post effects, surroundings and render scale (M3.4, D34). Old game left, new right: `echo-isles-water` (shallow channels), `echo-isles-coast`, `echo-isles-border` (map corner, turned 45°), `echo-isles-a` (player 1 start), `turtle-rock-water`, `turtle-rock-border` (west edge). New only: `echo-isles-ripples` (12 synthetic bodies wading, `--movers 12`), `echo-isles-forest` (the forest beyond the border in a free 22° view; no play or reference-stage camera can frame it), `echo-isles-a-contact` (contact shading off | on), `echo-isles-a-deep-forest-effects` and `…-autumn-forest-effects` (the map previewed in those biomes, haze and bloom off | on; no shipped map uses them, so there is no old capture), `render-scale-1-vs-075` (2560×1600 at render scale 1 | 0.75).
- `m3-water/`: water, shorelines and map borders; new-only images show ripples, contact shadows, haze/bloom and render scale.
- `m3-live/`: the live world (D35). `live-echo-p1-t800.png` and `live-echo-p2-t2400.png`: the old game's live match (left; player 1 idle human, player 2 AI, fog lifted, HUD hidden, captured headless through Chrome's DevTools protocol at the same tick) and ours at the same tick and hour 9.5 (right; both players AI). Player 2's base, buildings, workers and scaffold agree; the old frame has the construction dust and the mine label, ours not yet (effects, world UI). `battle-lab-vs-crowd.png`: the old combat lab's 24 vs 24 mixed army (left; its own camera and stage) next to our eight-player crowd battle (right), for the units only. `live-echo-battle-t16000.png`: an AI-versus-AI fight at tick 16,000 (the old match against an idle human never fought in 48,000 ticks, so there is no old battle at the same tick). `crowd-400-t120.png`: eight players × 50 animated soldiers (the stress view). `crowd-white.png`: the white player close up.
- `m4-play/`: the playable match (D36), new only, `canopy shot --play` on echo-isles: `workers-groups-build-menu` (five workers selected, control groups 1 and 2, the build menu with Back in slot 9), `placement-valid` (a house ghost on open ground) and `placement-blocked` (a barracks turned 90° against the trees), `hero` (the Marshal: stats, vitals, inventory, the spell banner), `hall-training` (the hall training workers: active task and waiting wells), `observer` (the spectator's colony overview at tick 2,600). Rings, markers and the footprint are the screen-space stand-in until the world UI pass.
