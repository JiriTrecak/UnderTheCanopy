Download for Mac macOS on Apple Silicon (M1 or newer)

Installing

  1. Open the downloaded file; macOS unpacks it into Under the Canopy.
  2. Drag Under the Canopy into your Applications folder and open it.
  3. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

After that the game keeps itself up to date: it offers each new version when it starts.
